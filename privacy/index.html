Who we are

Go Farva is made by Fez Commerce, a sole trader based in Sunderland, United Kingdom. Contact: robert@fezcommerce.co.uk.

What the app stores on your device

Runs, GPS points, settings, routes you create, and optional audio or plan preferences are stored locally on your phone (Room database and app files). We do not operate Go Farva accounts or our own servers that store your runs.

Location

While you are recording a run, the app uses precise location (GPS) in the foreground, and may use a foreground location service so tracking continues when the screen is off. Location is written into the run on your device. We do not upload your location to Fez Commerce.

Maps (OpenFreeMap / IP)

When you view a map online, map tiles are fetched from OpenFreeMap (OpenStreetMap data). That request typically includes your IP address and the map area you are viewing. Attribution: © OpenStreetMap contributors · OpenFreeMap. Offline map display may show a route-only fallback without tiles.

Pro / Play Billing

Pro is a one-off unlock (£4.99) sold through Google Play Billing. Purchase and entitlement are handled by Google. Fez Commerce does not receive your full payment card details. Google may process purchase data under its own policies.

Sharing

  • GPX and Garmin FIT: You can export a route or run file and share it yourself via the Android share sheet. There is no automatic sync to Garmin Connect or Strava.
  • Health Connect: Optional and write-only. If you enable it, finished runs can be written to Health Connect. The app does not read Health Connect data. See also the Health Connect privacy note.
  • Strava ZIP import: You can import a Strava export ZIP you choose. Import runs on your device; we do not connect to Strava’s API for sync.
  • Share cards: Images you generate for social media stay on your device until you share them.

No account / no own run servers

There is no Go Farva sign-up. We do not host a cloud copy of your run history. Uninstalling the app removes local data unless you have exported it.

Contact

Questions about privacy: robert@fezcommerce.co.uk.

Changes

We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Material changes will be reflected in the app listing / in-app link where required.
