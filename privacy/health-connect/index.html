Health Connect privacy

Optional write-only integration for Go Farva on Android.

Go Farva can optionally write finished runs to Android Health Connect so other apps you choose can see them.

  • Write-only. The app does not read exercise sessions, heart rate, steps, or any other Health Connect data.
  • Opt-in. Nothing is written until you enable Health Connect in Settings and grant permission in the system UI.
  • You control it. You can turn the setting off or revoke Health Connect permissions in Android settings at any time.
  • On-device. Fez Commerce does not receive Health Connect data from this feature.

Full privacy details for the app: Privacy policy. Questions: robert@fezcommerce.co.uk.
