Health Connect privacy

Optional write-only integration for Go Farva on Android.

App: Go Farva · Fez Commerce, Sunderland, UK
Contact: support@gofarva.run
Full privacy policy: gofarva.run/privacy

What we write (optional, off by default)

If you turn on Send runs to Health Connect in Settings, Go Farva may write to Health Connect on this phone, for each finished run you recorded here:

  • an exercise session (type: running), with start and end time and title;
  • the route (GPS points), if you allow it;
  • the distance.

What we never do

  • We do not read Health Connect data.
  • We do not send imported Strava runs to Health Connect.
  • We do not upload Health Connect data to any server of ours.
  • We do not sync runs to Strava or Garmin through Health Connect (they do not receive runs that way).

Your controls

  • Turn the setting off in Go Farva → Settings.
  • Revoke access in the Health Connect app.
  • Manage or delete past entries in Health Connect itself.

Why we ask

So other fitness apps you choose can see the runs you recorded in Go Farva, if you allow them in Health Connect.
