FAQ

Straight answers.

How do I join the Android closed test?

Go Farva is not on Google Play for everyone yet. Join the testers group for the closed Android beta: https://groups.google.com/g/go-farva-beta.

Does it need a signal?

No. Tracking uses GPS only. Map tiles need a connection when you first view an area; routes you plan can be used offline once the routing data is on your phone.

Do I need an account?

No sign-up, no login. Runs are stored on your device. There are no Go Farva run servers.

What does £4.99 get me?

A one-off Pro unlock through Google Play. No subscription, no ads. Pro adds stats, the route builder, watch export (GPX and FIT via the share sheet), the 9-week run/walk plan, Strava export ZIP import, and more. Importing single GPX, FIT or TCX files is free.

Does it sync with Strava or Garmin?

No automatic sync. You can import a Strava ZIP export into the app, and you can share a route as GPX or FIT to load on your watch yourself. Health Connect is optional and write-only: if you turn it on, finished runs can be written to Health Connect — nothing is read back.
